# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with GSAP and ScrollTrigger (vendored locally). Montserrat variable woff2 self-hosted in /fonts. No framework, no build step.

## Users

Buyers and decision makers at companies and governments in Uruguay and the region who need specialised printed products (labels, security documents, forms, promotional systems) or document and billing software (electronic invoicing, cross media, content management). They arrive to judge whether Garino is a serious, trustworthy supplier, then to contact the company or use a client service.

Secondary audience, for this project: the Garino management team, to whom the restyling is presented as a commercial proposal. The first screen has to impress them.

## Product Purpose

GARINO HNOS. S.A. is a Montevideo printing and IT-services company founded in 1920 by the brothers Guillermo and Américo Garino, still family-owned. Two divisions: Servicios Gráficos (high-specialisation printing under international quality and security standards) and Servicios Informáticos. The website presents both divisions and routes clients to contact, to the CFE lookup and to client portals. Success: a visitor concludes this is a first-tier, century-old, reliable supplier, and the proposal gets bought by the company.

## Positioning

Over 100 years of continuity in one family, combined with continuous reinvestment in technology and an internationally certified quality and security system (ISO 9001:2015, PS 9000:2016 for pharma labels). Seriousness and trust, not startup energy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three pages: home, servicios gráficos, servicios informáticos, with coherent nav and style.
- Palette is the current site's navy/blue family: #002b5c, #0065a4, #536283, #c6cbd6, #555555 on white. No new hues.
- Typography: Montserrat only, self-hosted variable woff2, no serif. Headings 700 to 800.
- Scroll interaction with GSAP/ScrollTrigger (reveals, subtle parallax, a pinned or horizontal-scroll section), honouring prefers-reduced-motion.
- Responsive at 390px, tablet and desktop with no accidental horizontal scroll.
- Informatics sub-pages (Nuestra Empresa, Factura Electrónica, etc.) could not be read from the live site; only the informatics home text and menu are available as real content.

## Brand Commitments

Real company copy is kept; nothing is invented. Seriousness of a centenary firm. The "100 años" identity and the founding year 1920 are central brand facts.

## Product Principles

- Century of trust first: the founding year and continuity are the strongest proof.
- Say only what Garino says; keep copy faithful to the company's own text.
- Precision as the visual language of a print house: registration, alignment, exact edges.
- Motion explains and reveals; it never decorates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion; content visible without JavaScript; AA contrast; Spanish (es-UY) throughout.
